# Sample SME Operations SOP & Escalation Protocol

## 1. Client Record Lookup
- Always verify client `status_note` and `last_active_year` before quoting renewal or service packages.
- If a client has multiple legacy IDs, refer to the unified master record ID.

## 2. Service & Parts Escalation
- Standard parts quote turnaround: within 4 business hours.
- If an item is marked `out_of_stock` across all branches, escalate to Procurement Lead with part code and vehicle model.
